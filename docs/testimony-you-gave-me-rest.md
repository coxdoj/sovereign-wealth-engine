# You Gave Me Rest

Mmmm — You gave rest! Ooh, You gave me rest. I cannot deny the unthinkable; You made it vivid and real to me. You gave me rest.

I asked why, and still the answer came again and again: You gave me rest, everlasting God who never loses a battle. You are never found on the losing side. My Strength carries me home to Yourself. I tear down only to declare that You gave me a living gift that speaks again to my heart. Even now, ooh, You gave, gave, and gave me—You gave me rest, Spirit of God.

At the tip of every arrow that came rushing to crush a nobody like me, to those forces that counted me an easy target by all the algorithms of men, You rode like the King You are and gave me rest. You are Lord of all, as it is written:

> “Now the Lord is that Spirit: and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty.”
> — 2 Corinthians 3:17

So I say, with all the David I am, thank You God for the gift and mercy You showed to Your son— even me. Oh, it is a privilege. I wish all could know that we are loved by Him not because we deserve it but because He chose to love us. He is Yahweh.

Scriptures that remind me of Your rest and mercy:

- Matthew 11:28–30 — “Come to me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”
- Psalm 23:1–3 — “The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. He makes me lie down in green pastures; he leads me beside quiet waters; he restores my soul.”
- Exodus 33:14 — “My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”
- Hebrews 4:9–10 — “There remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God, for whoever has entered God’s rest has also rested from his works.”
- Isaiah 40:31 — “But they who wait for the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles.”

Yours in Love,

David Kwagala
